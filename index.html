Where does Jev pull the lever?

Jev is a decision model: it answers with a choice and how likely it is, not with prose. Change one thing in a trolley problem, ask it every time, and watch the probability move. Say where you would tip first, then see where Jev does.

Inspired by Zach Landes' Jev Meets the Trolley Problem. Every answer comes from Pollinations; you pay with your own Pollen, and this page has no server and keeps no key. Jev is a paid-only model, so the account that signs in needs paid Pollen.
